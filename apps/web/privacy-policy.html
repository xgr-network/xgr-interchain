Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Applies to xita.xgr.network.

This XITA-specific policy supplements the general XGR.Network GmbH Privacy Policy and explains additional processing for XITA Join applications, emails and wallet-driven interchain transfers.

For privacy inquiries, please contact info@xgr.network.

2. Website hosting and security

Our server infrastructure uses STRATO. Technical requests may process IP address, date and time, requested URL, referrer, user agent, and HTTP status to deliver the service, maintain reliability, prevent abuse and investigate incidents. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests). Logs are retained only as necessary or where legally required. XITA does not run marketing trackers, advertising cookies or a newsletter.

3. Wallets and public blockchain information

Connecting your self-custodial wallet may expose its public wallet address to this interface. Transaction approval and signing are handled by wallet software. XITA does not request or retain private keys or seed phrases and does not take custody of your cryptoassets through the transfer interface. Public addresses, transaction hashes, amounts, events and proof records may be available to blockchain RPC providers and permanently recorded on public networks. The operator cannot generally erase blockchain records. Required interface processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

4. Join applications and status emails

To propose a CoinGecko-listed ERC-20 token, an applicant supplies public project information, the original chain and token contract, CoinGecko ID, desired routes, a contact email address and a declared relationship to the token (founder, team member, authorized representative, holder, community member or other). The email is used for application confirmation and updates relating directly to review, deployment, first independently verified token locking/TVL and public-listing status. We do not send marketing emails based on Join applications.

After the email has been confirmed, public project information and the applicant role category may be included in a GitHub draft pull request. The private contact email and confirmation token are not included in public GitHub PRs or token configuration files. Email confirmation does not establish token ownership or authority to represent the token project.

The contact email and internal application record are stored in a restricted server-side location outside Git and the webroot. One-time confirmation tokens are stored only as cryptographic hashes and expire after 24 hours. Expired unconfirmed requests are deleted. Active application records are generally retained for up to 366 days, and rejected or completed/publicly listed contact records for 180 days after the relevant event, subject to mandatory retention obligations.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR when processing is necessary for requested pre-contractual steps, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests in verifying applications, preventing abuse, ensuring reliable project reviews and communicating relevant status changes). Applicants may contact us to request deletion or to stop future updates, subject to overriding legal duties.

5. Transaction-linked fee and geographical evidence (planned service)

Where and only where XITA implements a transaction-linked fee or tax-evidence feature, the service may record a request's IP address at the time of a commercially relevant fee quote and associate it with the public wallet address, quote identifier, confirmed source transaction, amount of service fee, time, source and destination chain, derived country code and geo-IP provider/version. A country derived from an IP address is an estimate and is not definitive proof of tax residence, customer status or the place of supply. VPNs, proxies and shared access can produce inaccurate results. An on-chain transaction performed without a request to XITA cannot reliably be attributed to an HTTP IP address and will be treated as unknown.

Purpose: reconciling paid services, preventing duplicate or fraudulent fee claims and, only where legally justified and necessary, keeping proportionate evidence for bookkeeping and legally required tax classification. Possible legal bases may include Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR for applicable statutory retention/tax duties and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for proportionate fraud-prevention, subject to documented necessity and balancing. An optional feature must not be enabled until its actual data fields, lawful basis, access controls, host/provider processing, retention and deletion intervals, third-country transfers, and privacy impact have been reviewed and reflected here. Full raw IP addresses should not be included in public source-control, public API responses or on-chain data. Geo-IP estimates cannot be used alone to infer all VAT liabilities, including the separate tax treatment of validator remuneration.

Current status: This describes a planned feature, not a representation that XITA already persistently stores transaction-linked IP addresses. Specific retention periods for the feature require approval before activation.

6. Service providers and recipients

  • STRATO: hosting and related technical access records.
  • Zoho Mail: application confirmation and transactional status emails (Zoho Corporation GmbH, Essen, Germany).
  • GitHub: public token/project data, declared applicant role category and code-review records, without private contact email.
  • CoinGecko: verification of the submitted public coin profile and original token identity.
  • Public RPC/explorer providers: blockchain queries and associated connection metadata.

Where personal data is processed outside the EU/EEA, legally required transfer safeguards are used.

7. Your rights

Under Arts. 15–21 GDPR, subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and object to processing. Where consent is the legal basis, you may withdraw it prospectively. You can lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. Contact: info@xgr.network.

8. Updates and external services

Wallet providers and linked sites have independent privacy notices. Significant changes to XITA's processing will be reflected in an updated policy and revision date.
