Understand XITA: interchain transfers and project onboarding

Answers to common questions about supported routes, two-hop transfers, token representations, project approval and fees.

How does XITA work?

XGRChain connects independently deployed and verified routes between supported blockchain networks. Two external networks require separate source-to-XGRChain and XGRChain-to-destination transfers.

Do configured routes mean transfers are live?

No. Deployment, counterpart registration and execution readiness must be independently verified before a route is offered for real transfers.

Can projects join at no deployment cost?

Approved XITA Alliance routes receive XGR-sponsored technical integration and initial contract deployment. Network transaction fees and approval conditions remain separate.

What happens if a transfer is delayed?

Do not repeat a confirmed source transaction. Use its verified transaction status and available recovery process; delivery is not guaranteed.
