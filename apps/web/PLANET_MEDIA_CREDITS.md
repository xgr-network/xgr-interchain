# Background planet – source and attribution

The decorative planet on XITA subpages uses two locally hosted NASA-derived texture images, without loading external images at runtime.

- `planet-day.jpg`: NASA Blue Marble, cloud-free day composite; credit **Reto Stöckli**, with enhancements by **Robert Simmon**, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / NASA Earth Observatory. Original source: https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57730/land_ocean_ice_2048.jpg
- `planet-night.jpg`: NASA Black Marble 2016 nighttime-lights composite; credit **Joshua Stevens**, using Suomi NPP VIIRS data from **Miguel Román**, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / NASA Earth Observatory. Source: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/144000/144897/BlackMarble_2016_01deg_gray.jpg (local grayscale downsample at 2048×1024).

The images were sourced as frozen NASA-media derivatives from the public ORBITAL BLOOM asset inventory (https://github.com/Depal-Jakub/orbital-bloom/blob/main/DATA_SOURCES.md). They are displayed with additional CSS shading and atmospheric overlays; they are **decorative illustrations**, not current Earth observations or a geographical data service.

NASA has not reviewed, sponsored or endorsed XITA. No NASA name, insignia, seal or logo is used to suggest endorsement. NASA's media usage guidance: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/.
