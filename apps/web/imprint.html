About the XITA service

XITA is a discovery and transfer interface for publicly deployed interchain smart contracts. XGR.Network GmbH does not take custody of users' cryptoassets through the transfer interface, require wallet seed phrases or hold their private keys. Transfers are authorized using users' own wallets and involve network, smart-contract and liquidity risks. Use is at your own risk; statutory rights remain unaffected.
