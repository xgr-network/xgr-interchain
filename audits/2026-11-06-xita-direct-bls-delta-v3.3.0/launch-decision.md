# Separate operating decisions

These opinions apply only to the exact frozen source and observed evidence. They neither change production nor accept new policy risks for the operator. Missing host evidence alone is not a NO-GO.

## Self-funded Direct-BLS core transfers

**CONDITIONAL GO**

No new principal-authorization, token-backing or message-forgery regression was demonstrated in the reviewed delta. Retain authenticated route/asset/ISM/current-set bindings, the configured native finality policy, supported native-recipient restrictions and supervised recovery/backlog controls. Correlated RPC witnesses remain a stated trust dependency. This is not approval for arbitrary code-bearing native recipients or unlimited load.

## Regular unrestricted unattended optional sponsorship

**NO-GO**

F-06 and F-33 are now verified source closures; they are no longer the blockers. F-08 and F-19 still prevent a reliable all-chain autonomous servicing and controlled paid-obligation outcome claim. Immutable liabilities are preserved, but preservation alone is not fulfillment. This decision is based on source behavior, not missing per-host attestations.

## Staffed, bounded sponsorship and recovery

**CONDITIONAL GO**

Requires an explicitly approved operator-owned outcome/escalation process for outstanding obligations, effective restricted-role migrations, fair/backlog monitoring, exact transaction/nonce preservation and a separately authorized operating plan. No such new policy acceptance or production enablement is supplied by this audit. Never delete an uncertain obligation or re-sign it solely to recover availability.

## Public unattended Token Creator intake

**NO-GO for an unattended availability claim**

F-36 is a freshly reproduced application-to-worker failure-isolation defect. An admitted unprocessable item can prevent healthy queued work from progressing. Correct validation and per-item failure isolation before claiming autonomous public intake. Existing correctly deployed tokens are not invalidated, and unauthorized token ownership or minting was not demonstrated.

## Supervised Creator operation

**CONDITIONAL GO**

Only under an explicit operator-controlled intake/recovery procedure that avoids the demonstrated blocking condition and preserves every uncertain deployment identity. This does not authorize deletion of unresolved journal records. Address F-37/F-38 recovery/provenance limitations and F-39 misleading presentation.

## XGR node v3.5.0 possession delta

**CONDITIONAL GO within the reviewed delta**

The known rogue-key admission cause is verified remediated on the frozen source. Fresh build/package/fork/import tests and read-only runtime/activation observations are positive. No additional Critical or High defect was demonstrated. Preserve the already activated canonical fork/manifest and normal quorum rules; do not repeat migration, restake or roll back to a pre-fix binary. This is not a whole-node audit or an attestation of every installed executable.
