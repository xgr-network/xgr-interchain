# Remaining conditions and remediation - r1

Follow report.md sections 7-8; the conditions are prerequisites for the restricted recommendation, not claims of completed production controls.

| Gate | Owner | Required control and re-evaluation trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | Validator operators / network operator | Corrected code and canonical fork schedule. Preserve build/configuration evidence, monitor root agreement. Reassess on incompatible roots, nonconforming quorum or accounting bypass. |
| G2 | XITA service operator | Assessed routes only; supported code-free EOA native recipients. No invented extra claim signature. Reassess on new chains, token behavior, recipient policy or deployed bindings. |
| G3 | Sponsor / treasury operator | No new sponsor promises in approved self-funded scope. Preserve old liabilities and nonce ownership; use immutable age/error evidence and controlled reconciliation. Never clear ambiguous commitments to free capacity. Sponsorship remains outside approval. |
| G4 | Node/RPC and service operators | Bound public resource admission, preserve consensus capacity, monitor disk/backlog, retain original recovery evidence. Restrict new admissions on sustained backlog or resource growth. |
| G5 | Validator / deployment operators | Maintain committee/quorum, independent keys and witnesses, and one active registry per chain. Reassess provider correlation, set changes or registry migrations. |

F-07/F-19 are not fixed by liability monitoring. F-08 is only partially isolated. F-30 can suppress age-based delayed-grant alerts, so a clean diagnostic alone cannot establish absence of liabilities. F-03/F-04/F-20/F-21/F-29/F-32 require bounded operating controls before scaling. F-05 remains Medium architectural residual risk.

Unrestricted GO requires evidence of operating controls, defensible resolution/containment of paid obligations, reliable age/error monitoring, representative paid/self-funded journeys, and closure or explicit technically bounded owner acceptance of remaining material risks. No broad protocol redesign, extra user signature or bridge redeployment is mandated solely to remove every Medium/Low item.
